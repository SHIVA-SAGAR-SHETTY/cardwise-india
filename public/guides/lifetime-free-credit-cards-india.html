Best Lifetime Free Credit Cards in India (2026)

Updated 23 September 2026 · Compares 7 cards

A lifetime-free (LTF) credit card has no joining fee and no annual fee, ever. That makes it a safe first card, a good backup card, and a way to keep your credit history long without paying to hold the card. Keeping a card open is good for your credit score, because the average age of your accounts matters.

Free doesn't have to mean useless. Several lifetime-free cards in India offer real rewards, zero forex markup, or even lounge access. Here are the best ones right now.

Lifetime free vs first-year free

Some cards advertise themselves as 'free' but only waive the fee for the first year. BOBCARD Eterna, for example, stopped being lifetime free in September 2026 and now charges ₹2,499 from year two unless you spend ₹2.5 lakh. Always check the annual (renewal) fee, not just the joining fee.

How to choose

  • You shop on Amazon: Amazon Pay ICICI.
  • You travel abroad: Scapia Federal or IDFC FIRST (zero forex).
  • You want lounges without paying a fee: IndusInd Tiger.
  • You pay mostly by UPI: Kiwi Axis RuPay.

Frequently asked questions

Do lifetime-free cards hurt my credit score?

No. Like any card, applying causes a hard enquiry, but holding a free card for years helps your score by lengthening your credit history and increasing your available credit.

Can a bank start charging a fee on a lifetime-free card?

Banks can change terms with notice, as BOBCARD did with Eterna in 2026. It's rare for cards sold as lifetime free, and you can always close the card if a fee is introduced.
